## Shannon entropy

For a discrete random variable, Shannon entropy summarizes uncertainty as the weighted negative logarithm of outcome probabilities. A distribution concentrated on one outcome has no uncertainty, while a more even distribution has greater uncertainty.

The familiar expression is H(X) = -sum p(x) log p(x). The base of the logarithm determines the unit, such as bits for base two.
